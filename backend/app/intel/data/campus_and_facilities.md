# JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU) Campus, Facilities, Placements & Contact Information

## Campus Location and Infrastructure
- **Location**: Near Mahindra World City, P.O. Mahapura, Ajmer Road, Jaipur, Rajasthan 302026, India.
- **Campus Area**: 30-acre lush green, eco-friendly, fully Wi-Fi enabled residential campus.
- **Academic Facilities**: Modern smart classrooms, high-performance computing labs, AI & Robotics innovation center, Maker Space, Design Studios, and a multi-storey central library.
- **Sports & Recreation**: Cricket and football grounds, basketball and volleyball courts, indoor badminton arena, gymnasium, and student recreation center.
- **Healthcare**: 24x7 on-campus health clinic with visiting doctor, resident nurse, and emergency ambulance service.

## Placements and Industry Partnerships
- **Highest Package (Engineering & Technology)**: INR 22.5 LPA.
- **Average Package (B.Tech CSE / AI)**: INR 7.8 LPA.
- **Top Recruiters**: Infosys, TCS, Cognizant, IBM, L&T Technology Services, JK Tyre, JK Paper, Safe Security, Celebal Technologies, Hexaview Technologies.
- **Academic Partnerships**: Academic collaborations with University of Florida, Edinburgh Napier University, and St. Cloud State University for semester exchange and study abroad programs.
