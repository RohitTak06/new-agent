# JK Lakshmipat University (JKLU) Campus, Facilities, Placements and Contact Information

Source: jklu.edu.in contact, placements, campus life and lateral entry pages, checked on 7 November 2026.

## Campus Location and Infrastructure
- **Location**: Near Mahindra SEZ, Mahapura, Ajmer Road, Jaipur 302026, Rajasthan, India, about 25 km from the heart of Jaipur city.
- **Campus Area**: 30 acres of greenery.
- **Accreditation**: Accredited with an 'A' grade by NAAC.
- **Academic Facilities**: Advanced labs, design studios, a makerspace, the Learning Resource Centre and the MP Ranjan Design Resource Centre.

## Hostels
Separate fully furnished hostels for boys and girls, with common lounges, a TV room, and indoor spaces for table tennis, chess and carrom.

## Mess and Eateries
The campus mess provides nutritious, hygienic meals that cater to diverse dietary needs. The mess fee is listed with the hostel fees.

## Sports and Fitness
Sports facilities include basketball, volleyball, chess and carrom, and there is a fitness centre on campus.

## Health and Wellness
- **Health Centre**: A medical centre with qualified doctors and healthcare professionals available around the clock, with regular health check-ups.
- **Ambulance**: A 24x7 ambulance service for emergencies.
- **Counselling**: Mindery, an on-campus and online confidential counselling service for students.

## Placements and Industry Partnerships
- **Highest Package (CTC)**: 31 LPA.
- **Average Package (CTC)**: 8.8 LPA.
- **Median Package (CTC)**: 7 LPA.
- **Top 10% Average Package (CTC)**: 18.07 LPA.
- **Scope**: Placement highlights for students from Engineering, Management and Design, not split by programme here.
- **Top Recruiters and Placement Reports**: The list of recruiters and the placement reports for B.Tech, BBA and B.Des are published on the JKLU placements page.

## International Collaborations and Semester Exchange
- **MoUs with international institutions**: University of Massachusetts Amherst, University of Birmingham (UK), St. Cloud State University (USA), Hogeschool van Amsterdam (Netherlands) and Edinburgh Napier University (UK).
- **Semester exchange**: B.Tech students can spend up to four semesters at institutions such as various IITs, IIITs and international universities.
- **Support**: The Office of International Student Affairs advises students on exchange programmes and international opportunities.
